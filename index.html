In short

Camperleveling does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app has no internet access, contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and needs no user account.

Accelerometer

The app reads your device's accelerometer to calculate the tilt. The readings are processed in real time on the device only and are neither stored nor shared.

Locally stored settings

Vehicle dimensions, ramp heights, color limits, language, display smoothing and the zero point are stored on your device only. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
